# Фотографии для ELARA

Для каталога нужны **24 снимка**, по одному на модель. На каждый дополнительный цвет отдельный файл не нужен. Для главной страницы можно прислать **ещё один** горизонтальный снимок.

Удобный вариант для карточек — квадратные JPEG от 800 × 800 пикселей, чтобы товар целиком помещался в кадре. Для обложки подойдёт JPEG около 1500 × 1000 пикселей.

Готовые файлы можно положить поверх демоснимков в `resources/images/products/`. Имена из таблицы должны совпасть. Для обложки используется `resources/images/campaign/autumn-winter.jpg`. После замены перезапусти магазин.

<summary>Как сделаны демонстрационные фотографии</summary>

Встроенный генератор изображений создал четыре листа по шесть предметных снимков и одну обложку. Листы разрезаны на отдельные JPEG по артикулам.

**Общая часть запроса для четырёх листов**

Create one exact 3-column × 2-row contact sheet of six distinct original photorealistic high-end fashion ecommerce product photographs. Product-mockup use case for the ELARA desktop clothing store. Landscape 3:2 composition. The centers of the panels must be at exact 1/6, 1/2, 5/6 X and 1/4, 3/4 Y. Each panel will be cut into an independent square photograph at exactly those thirds and halfway down; keep at least 13% margin around every subject in its cell. Strictly one subject per cell (a matching pair where specified). Each cell is the same pale, warm studio sweep backdrop (#e9e3dc) with naturally soft contact shadows and directional studio light. Premium restrained art direction, rich tactile details and true-to-life colors. All items fully visible and clearly distinct. No grid lines, text, lettering, boxes, framing, humans, hands, mannequins, logos, watermarks, or brand marks. Top row left to right, then bottom row left to right:

**Женское**

(1) cropped warm ivory double-breasted wool jacket with an elegant pointed collar and dark buttons; (2) merlot red bias-cut satin midi slip dress with slender wide-set straps; (3) long-sleeved pearl silk blouse with softly draping cuffs and a minimalist collar; (4) warm sand-colored pressed pleat midi skirt in fine suiting wool; (5) full-length dark indigo straight-leg high-waisted jeans with subtle tobacco stitching; (6) long-sleeved fitted black ribbed knit midi dress, mock neck. Ghost product display, convincing volume. All six garments completely contained in their tiles, photographed front-on.

**Мужское**

(1) men's dark espresso single-breasted wool overcoat with notched lapel; (2) pale blue crisp poplin button-down shirt, long sleeves; (3) charcoal straight-legged wool trousers with sharp front pleats; (4) pair of elegant dark brown leather penny loafers on a matte neutral studio surface in three-quarter view; (5) heavy, bright ecru short-sleeve cotton crew-neck T-shirt; (6) olive suede men's zip-up bomber jacket. Ghost product display or natural flat lay, crisp realistic tailoring, all garments completely contained in their dedicated tiles.

**Сумки и аксессуары**

(1) merlot rectangular structured leather evening clutch with a brushed brass tab clasp, with no logos; (2) compact dark chocolate leather backpack with minimal stitched construction and a softly arched top; (3) slim closed black grained leather bifold wallet lying at a slight angle; (4) cognac leather belt neatly coiled, rectangular satin brass buckle facing camera; (5) pair of thick polished amber tortoiseshell rectangular sunglasses with dark lenses, folded temples; (6) milk-ivory oval sculptural leather clutch with curved seam. Three-quarter views. Full silhouettes.

**Украшения**

(1) sculpted broad flowing gold wave ring resting on warm pale limestone; (2) pair of small wide gold half-hoop earrings, simple sculptural forms; (3) thin gold chain and round black pearl pendant arranged in an elegant wide oval, full chain in one tile; (4) open brushed sterling silver cuff bracelet with a subtle knot-like bend at center; (5) small rectangular wristwatch, cream dial, subtle Roman numerals, black leather strap, no watch brand; (6) broad brushed silver signet ring with flat polished black onyx oval inlay. Macro product photography, exquisite texture.

**Обложка**

Create one wide 3:2 photorealistic fashion campaign photograph for an independent contemporary clothing store, used in the right-hand picture of a split editorial homepage hero. Italian modernist interior with bronzed stone and warm neutral travertine, strong low sun from the right, unhurried mood, subtle film grain and cream, oxblood, cocoa color family. Stylish adult woman on left in an ivory double breasted boxy jacket and dark indigo jeans, holding a merlot leather clutch at her side. Stylish adult man beside her on the right in a tailored dark espresso wool overcoat over a cream shirt, looking beyond camera. Both models framed mid-thigh and up, natural editorial poses, faces and shoulders in the central safe 70% of the image, visibly textured fabric, polished independent fashion magazine photography. Scene fully fills wide frame. No lettering, banners, logos, watermarks, text overlay, grids, collages.
